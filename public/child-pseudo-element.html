Child and Pseudo-Element

A fixed element at the top or the bottom edge often has more than one color in it: its own background, plus a child or a ::before on top. Which one does Safari use for the status bar and the toolbar?

Colors: blue page, red fixed element, yellow child or ::before. Pick a case and compare the bars with the status bar and the toolbar.

Rules

  • Safari uses the color of the fixed element.
  • A child supplies the color only when the element has no color, the child covers the center of the edge, and it is at least 90% as wide as the screen. A narrower child has no effect.
  • A ::before or ::after counts as part of its element. Safari sees its color only by sampling pixels, and only when the bar is fully on screen and at least 6px tall. A narrow bar is partly off screen, so a ::before on it has no effect.
